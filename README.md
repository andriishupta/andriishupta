# Andrii Shupta

Andrii Shupta is a Software Consultant and Developer with 10 years of experience helping product teams turn ambiguous product and technical problems into reliable, maintainable systems. His work combines architecture and system design, full-stack development, technical direction, code and architecture review, and production delivery. He works pragmatically with technologies such as TypeScript, JavaScript, React, Next.js, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Supabase, cloud platforms, third-party APIs, and applied AI. The guiding principle is simple: choose the technology and level of complexity that fit the product, team, and constraints.
